📚 FAQ

How do I reset my password?
Go to Settings → Account → Reset Password. A reset link will be sent to your email.

Why are prices not updating?
Ensure the backend server is running on port 8003. Prices refresh every 5-10 seconds automatically.

How do I delete my account?
Click your profile dropdown → Delete Account. This action is irreversible.
