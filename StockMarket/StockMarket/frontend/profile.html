Help & Support

How do I place a simulated trade?
Navigate to the "Stocks" section, search for a ticker, and click "Buy" or "Sell" to execute a paper trade using your virtual balance.

Why is the market closed?
Indian markets (NSE/BSE) operate Monday to Friday between 9:15 AM and 3:30 PM IST. Outside these hours, live price updates are paused.
