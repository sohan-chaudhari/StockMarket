01

The two cycles, as implemented

Verified against config/retention_policy.py (the only policy the app loads — the .yaml is dead config), retention_service.py, and the live candles table. The chains are genuinely independent: there is no 4h→1D and no 1h→1D rule anywhere in the code.

Intraday cycle

5m↓ after 60 sessions 15m↓ after 120 sessions 30m↓ after 180 sessions 1h↓ after 365 sessions 4h — terminal, 0 rows exist

Terminal at 4h by design. Verified: no rule anywhere promotes 4h onward. The 1h→4h rule has never fired — 1h history only reaches 2025-11-03, short of the 365-session threshold.

Long-term cycle

1D↓ after 730 calendar days 1W↓ after 1825 calendar days 1M — terminal, retained indefinitely

Fully independent, and observed working live: this session's cycle promoted 4,958 daily rows into 1W and 524 weekly into 1M. Uses calendar days, not sessions — deliberate and documented.

Boundary between the cycles (section 8 of your brief): there is none, and that is correct. 4h and 1D never touch. 1D is sourced entirely independently — by _daily_prefill_all, an automated 19:00 IST job that fetches daily candles for every ticker in stock_metadata. That is why the long-term chain is healthy while the intraday chain is starving: 1D has an automated universe-wide feed and intraday does not.

P0 · Implementation gapFEED-01

Universe-wide intraday ingestion collapsed on 2026-07-06

Through June, roughly 3,200 tickers received 5m candles every session. On 2026-07-06 that fell to 31 and has never recovered. 1h ingestion died a week later and is now effectively dead.

Universe-wide 5m coverage was never automated. It came from manually run backfill scripts (backfill_intraday_5min.py and siblings, among the 155 loose scripts in backend/). When those stopped being run, all that remained was the live AngelOne websocket — capped at 500 subscriptions with LRU rotation, reaching roughly 850 tickers. Compare _daily_prefill_all, which schedules itself daily at 19:00 IST for every ticker in stock_metadata: that is why 1D never wavered.

Fix The intraday chain needs the equivalent of _daily_prefill_all — a scheduled, universe-wide 5m fetch after market close, driven by the same stock_metadata list, with per-ticker job tracking. This is the single highest-value change in the platform. Until it exists, retention is compressing an incomplete dataset.
P0 · ConsequenceFEED-02

Retention will promote the holes into permanent history

This is the part that makes FEED-01 urgent rather than merely bad. Retention is working correctly — which is precisely the problem. It faithfully compresses whatever 5m data exists at the 60-session boundary. The July–August window currently sits at 4–27% universe coverage, and it is now roughly 30 sessions old.

Timeline
2026-07-06   5m coverage collapses to ~26% of universe
     now     that window is ~30 sessions old — still inside 5m retention
  ~Oct 26    it crosses 60 sessions → promoted to 15m, 5m rows deleted
             the gaps become permanent and unrecoverable in-system

There is a window — roughly 30 sessions — in which backfilling from the provider still repairs this cleanly. After that the source rows are gone and only the provider's own history could rebuild it, subject to AngelOne and yfinance retention limits.

Fix Backfill 2026-07-06 → present at 5m for the full universe before the retention boundary reaches it. Consider pausing the 5m→15m rule until coverage is restored.

Daily source of truth

P2 · Inconsistent precedenceSRC-01

Two daily stores with different roles and no declared precedence

Both hold daily data, and the split is defensible — but which one wins depends on which code path you enter through, and that is not intentional.

RELIANCE daily
stock_data    2,833 rows   2015-01-01 → 2026-06-25   deep history, stale tail
candles/1D      485 rows   2024-08-19 → 2026-08-17   fresh, 2-yr window

reader                          uses          consequence
ChartService "1D"               stock_data    ends 2026-06-25 (53d stale)
/api/stock-data/range/paginated stock_data    same
retention Chain B (1D→1W)       candles       archive built from 2 yrs only

The roles are coherent: stock_data is the deep import archive, candles/1D the live rolling window. The bug is that no reader unions them, so the chart shows a stale tail while fresh data sits one table over, and the weekly/monthly archive is built from the shallow source rather than the deep one.

Fix Declare candles canonical for reads, backfill stock_data's pre-2024 history into it at timeframe='1D', and keep stock_data as an immutable import record. Chain B then has eleven years to work with instead of two.
P1 · ReliabilityMIG-01

The 1D migration's checksum gate does not gate anything

migration_jobs is the mechanism your brief called migration_progress, and it implements the exact states you named. But its verification columns are inert.

migration_jobs — 3,281 rows, all tier 6 / 1D
KNOWN_ABSENCES  2240      DONE  932      FAILED  105      FETCHING  4

checksum_src IS DISTINCT FROM checksum_db  →  3,044 of 3,277 (93%)

stuck in FETCHING since 2026-08-14/15, retry_count = 0:
  DIVOPPBEES  DIVYADHAN  GKWLIMITED  GLAND

Jobs are marked DONE while their two checksums disagree, so the comparison is either never enforced or the two values are computed over different domains. Either way it provides no integrity guarantee. Separately, four jobs have sat in FETCHING for two to three days with no retry — unlike RetentionService, which does implement checkpoint resume, this migration has no stuck-job recovery.

Fix Either enforce the checksum (fail the job on mismatch) or remove the columns so they stop implying a guarantee that is not there. Add a startup sweep that resets stale FETCHING jobs to PENDING.
06

Findings that survived re-verification

P1 · Retention bugDATA-02

Batch boundaries silently corrupt one candle per 100 rows

_process_ticker resamples in batches of 100. A 15m bucket needs three 5m rows and 100 is not divisible by 3, so every boundary splits a bucket. The partial is inserted first; the correct full bucket arrives next batch and on_conflict_do_nothing discards it.

Reproduced against the real batching logic
bucket 2026-01-06 11:15     truth → stored
  high     206.0 → 204.0      close  202.0 → 200.0
  volume      30 → 10         (1 of 3 source candles)

The checksum cannot catch it — it compares source against target within one batch, which always agrees. Source rows are deleted afterwards, so the loss is unrecoverable.

Fix Extend each batch to the next target-bucket boundary, or switch to ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE so a fuller bucket supersedes a partial one.
P1 · Resampling bugDATA-03

4h offset is wrong, and 4h is unreachable anyway

NSE open 09:15 = 555 minutes
555 %  15 =  0   15m — no offset      ✓
555 %  30 = 15   30m — offset 15min   ✓
555 %  60 = 15   1h  — offset 15min   ✓
555 % 240 = 75   4h  — needs 75min, code uses 15

5m→4h, one session: first bucket = 08:15  (pre-market)

Compounding it: /api/stock-data/intraday/paginated rejects any interval outside 1m/5m/15m/30m/1h, so the terminal tier of your intraday cycle is write-only. Both are latent today — zero 4h rows exist — but both must land before the 1h→4h rule fires.

Fix Derive the offset: offset = f"{555 % bucket_minutes}min". Add 4h to the endpoint whitelist and the timeframe selector.

P1 · Implementation gapCAL-01

No holiday calendar loaded

Wiring is correct — startup reads holidays and feeds nse_calendar, the aggregator and special sessions. The table is empty, and so is market_sessions. Runtime confirms: [Startup] Loaded 0 NSE holidays.

Retention's _session_cutoff therefore counts holidays as trading sessions and trims ~10–14 days per year early; get_next_trading_day can queue an AMO onto a closed day. Not a trading-safety hole — on a holiday no ticks arrive, PriceProvider tiers 1–3 fail their staleness checks, tier 4 is gated on the market being closed, and the engine executes nothing.

Fix Populate holidays; scripts exist for 2024–2025 but not 2026. Register 2026-02-01 as a Budget special session — its 19,219 Sunday 1h rows are legitimate. The 1,988 Saturday rows on 2026-07-11 are a backfill artifact and should be deleted.

07

Verified working — do not change

Two-cycle retention model

Independent intraday and long-term chains, no cross-promotion, terminal tiers correct. Atomic insert→verify→delete, keyset pagination, per-ticker isolation, live-window protection, checkpoint resume. The design is right.

OHLC aggregation math

first/max/min/last/sum verified for every transition. 15m, 30m and 1h all bucket from 09:15 correctly. W-MON yields Monday-anchored weeks, MS month starts. Only 4h is wrong.

Session snapping

snap_to_nse_session computes the IST day boundary properly and handles pre-open, mid-session and post-close. The UTC-midnight class of bug is genuinely absent apart from the 15:30 edge.

Coordinate engine

225/225 exact bar round-trips. Out-of-range times extrapolate via a median edge interval; weekend gaps interpolate proportionally. The bar-index-as-timestamp bug is fixed in the core.

Drawing tool registry

108 tools: zero registry entries missing a class, zero declared-vs-actual point-count mismatches, zero orphans. Clean base-class hierarchy across lines, channels, pitchforks, Fibonacci, Gann, waves and patterns.

Execution engine

SELECT … FOR UPDATE locks, trigger evaluation inside the transaction, an EXECUTING state surviving crash-restart, and a market-hours guard that fails closed.

Price resolution

Four tiers with staleness ceilings (30s tick, 420s derived). Daily close reachable only when the market is closed. Degrades to no-price rather than a wrong price.

Subscription management

ADR-011 execution-pinned subscriptions with viewer and execution ref counts, DB-authoritative reconciliation, LRU eviction at 500 that never evicts a ticker with a live order.

Daily prefill

The one intraday-adjacent job that is automated, and it shows: 1D held ~3,150 tickers per session throughout the collapse. This is the pattern the 5m pipeline should copy.

Auth & tests

bcrypt, 5-attempt lockout, CSRF, rate limiting, audit log; .env gitignored and absent from history. 475 tests pass in 10.6s. Zero TODO/FIXME/HACK in core modules.

Phased roadmap

Fix now — before the retention boundary moves

  • Restore universe-wide 5m ingestion — a scheduled post-close job over stock_metadata, mirroring _daily_prefill_all. Everything else depends on this.
  • Backfill 2026-07-06 → present at 5m while the window is still inside the 60-session retention period. Consider pausing 5m→15m until coverage is restored.
  • Restore 1h ingestion — dead since 2026-07-13.
  • Fix the retention batch boundary — every cycle corrupts more candles and deletes the evidence.

Improve next

  • Populate the holiday calendar including 2026; register the Feb 1 special session.
  • Fix the 15:30 phantom bucket and purge the 976 rows.
  • Correct the 4h offset and expose 4h through the API — both before the 1h→4h rule fires.
  • Declare candles canonical for daily; migrate stock_data history in so Chain B has real depth.
  • Enforce or remove the migration checksum; add stale-FETCHING recovery.
  • Extend on-the-fly resampling to interior holes, not just the tip.

New capability — highest professional value

  • Data-coverage transparency. Surface, per timeframe, the range actually available and mark discontinuities on the chart. Given that resolution legitimately varies with age, a trader needs to see which resolution they are looking at. This turns a correct-but-opaque architecture into a trustworthy one.
  • Ingestion health monitoring — an alert when per-session ticker coverage drops below a threshold would have caught FEED-01 on 2026-07-06 instead of six weeks later.
  • Decide instrument identity (symbol vs. symbol+exchange) before BSE ingestion or options work begins. 2,278 symbols are dual-listed; candle tables have no exchange column.

Do not change

  • The two-cycle retention architecture. It is correct, deliberate, and my revision-1 criticism of it was wrong.
  • Terminal tiers at 4h and 1M. No 4h→1D promotion should be added.
  • The coordinate engine, tool registry, execution engine, PriceProvider and subscription manager.
  • Do not respond to this report by storing every timeframe at full resolution forever. The gaps are an ingestion failure, not evidence against retention.
